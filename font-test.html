How to layer for an upstate October.

Mornings are cold, afternoons are not, and nobody wants to carry a coat all day.

Ninth of June, October 2026

The trick is a few good pieces that work together. Start with something soft against the skin, add a sweater you could wear on its own, and finish with something that blocks the wind. Take one off when the sun comes out and you're done.

Start with the sweater

A wool sweater does most of the work. It's warm without being bulky, it looks good with jeans, and it keeps its shape. If you only buy one new thing this month, make it this.

Comfortable, but not lazy. Handsome, but not elitist.

What to bring on a walk through town

  • A sweater you love
  • Boots that can handle wet leaves
  • One layer you can tie around your waist
